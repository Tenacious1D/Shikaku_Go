## Pip sprite sheet

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: mobile puzzle game mascot sprite sheet
Primary request: Create a production-ready sprite sheet for Pip, a tiny friendly folded-paper map mascot for a calm cartography-themed Shikaku puzzle app.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background
Subject: the same simple mascot in six equal square cells arranged as a precise 3-column by 2-row grid: neutral welcome pose, teaching with one corner extended like a pointer, inspecting a tiny map with a small magnifier, offering a compass-token hint, celebrating with a tiny expedition flag, and a compact concerned “not quite” pose. Pip is a softly rectangular folded parchment map with one upturned folded corner, two small ink-dot eyes, a tiny curved mouth, subtle brass route lines, and short paper-tab arms. No legs are necessary.
Style/medium: clean polished 2D game UI character art, flat shapes with subtle paper grain and restrained soft shading; readable at 96 pixels
Composition/framing: each pose centered and fully contained in its equal cell with generous transparent padding; consistent proportions, face, palette, line weight, lighting, and scale across all six cells; no panel borders or dividers
Color palette: warm parchment, charcoal ink, muted brass, tiny moss and lake-blue accents
Constraints: genuinely transparent background and preserved alpha; no text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, extra characters, props beyond those specified, drop shadows extending outside cells, or cropped edges; exact 3 by 2 pose layout
Avoid: glossy 3D, anime, photorealism, childish baby proportions, busy detail, saturated rainbow colors

Style/medium: crisp flat 2D game icon with subtle tactile paper grain and restrained depth; vector-friendly silhouette
